About

I am a Staff AI Engineer and Researcher at the Itaú Institute of Science and Technology (ICTi), where I lead applied work in generative AI, especially text and voice agents for large, regulated environments, and lead AI research groups inside the institute. I hold an M.Sc. in Robotics from the Federal University of ABC (UFABC), a bachelor’s degree in Engineering from UFABC, and a bachelor’s degree in Science and Technology from UFABC. I spent part of my undergraduate studies at the University of Strathclyde (UK), and I completed a postgraduate specialization in Data Science at the Aeronautics Institute of Technology (ITA). This path lets me connect research, deployment and compliance, and I collaborate with researchers from leading universities such as MIT to keep the problems I work on aligned with current academic discussions. In my current position I lead research that results in publications and patentable AI solutions in security, evaluation and voice.

Research interests

My earlier research in robotics gave me experience with systems that need to perceive and respond reliably to their environment. My more recent work in biometrics and spoofing brought in the dimension of identity, security and evaluation. These two lines come together in my current focus on generative AI agents: I am interested in agents that can interact with humans and other agents, but also behave in a way that is verifiable and safe to deploy.

I am interested in agents that can interact with humans, with other agents and with their environment in a consistent and controllable way. My current focus is on agentic large language models that can keep long context, decide when to use tools or retrieval, and support voice interaction while still following safety and institutional policies. I have worked on research that resulted in publications and patentable AI solutions in security, evaluation and voice.

I am also interested in the next generation of agents that improve after deployment. I want to study how this kind of autonomous evolution can be done under clear safeguards, with behavior that can be measured and explained, so that organizations can adopt these agents at scale.

Selected projects

Ensemble Based Biometric Verification for Deepfake Resistance
Research and engineering effort that resulted in the 2025 paper published in Computers (MDPI). The work was carried out in collaboration with researchers from MIT and explored ensemble strategies to defend against multiple attack vectors in biometric scenarios, connecting academic methods with deployment oriented requirements. Link

ASV CM Score Fusion for Robust Speaker Verification
Applied research on speaker verification in spoofed environments. The study proposes fusion of automatic speaker verification and countermeasure scores to improve robustness in realistic conditions. The work was accepted and presented at BRACIS 2025 and will appear in the proceedings. This project was also awarded 2nd place in the Innovative Project category of the IEL Talent Award. Event page

RAG Voice Assistant for the community
Open source project to demonstrate practical uses of generative AI and retrieval augmented generation in a voice interface. The repository shows how to combine speech input, LLM reasoning and retrieval to answer in a natural way, aimed at helping other developers and students understand how to build end to end AI agents.
